# ASD-STE100 notes

Read this file when a user asks about ASD-STE100 sources, exact compliance, or the limits of this skill.

This skill borrows rule categories from ASD-STE100 Issue 9, published in January 2025. ASD developed Simplified Technical English for technical instructions that readers must interpret consistently.

Issue 9 contains 53 writing rules in nine sections. Its dictionary contains about 900 approved words with restricted meanings and parts of speech. It also lists about 1,200 words to avoid and permits project-specific technical terms.

The `unslop` skill does not include that dictionary. It can apply structural rules and terminology checks, but it cannot check exact approved vocabulary. Describe its output as controlled English or STE-inspired.

Reserve an ASD-STE100 compliance claim for a check of every applicable rule and word against the official standard and dictionary. For regulated or safety-critical documentation, use the official standard and the organization's review process.

Primary sources:

- [ASD-STE100 official site](https://www.asd-ste100.org/)
- [Official downloads](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html)
